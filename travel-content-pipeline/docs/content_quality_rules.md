# Content Quality Rules

## Purpose

These rules define the manual review standard for Explore plans generated by `travel-content-pipeline`.

They are intended for human QA, batch spot checks, and local review workflows.

## Review Dimensions

### 1. City specificity

Check whether the plan actually feels rooted in the destination.

Pass signal:

- Has local character, district texture, or place-based atmosphere
- Not limited to the most obvious landmarks
- Food and route choices reflect the city rather than generic tourism

Risk signal:

- Could be transplanted to another city with minimal edits
- Uses famous names only, with no local texture

### 2. Traveler fit

Check whether the plan matches the intended `trip_type`.

Pass signal:

- Pace, activity density, and tone match the traveler group
- POI choices reflect real needs of that audience
- Summary and itinerary are aligned with the same target user

Risk signal:

- “family” route feels exhausting
- “couple” route lacks atmosphere
- “student” route ignores budget sensitivity
- “solo” route feels inconvenient or overly dependent on group context

### 3. Route logic

Check whether the route is operationally reasonable.

Pass signal:

- Daily movement is geographically believable
- Time blocks feel plausible
- Activities follow a usable order
- Food suggestions fit the day’s area or timing

Risk signal:

- Frequent cross-city or cross-district jumping with no logic
- Overloaded days with too many heavy stops
- Meals detached from the route

### 4. Natural writing

Check whether the copy reads like thoughtful editorial content rather than a brittle template.

Pass signal:

- Smooth, readable Chinese
- Specific reasons instead of empty praise
- Little repetition across summary, itinerary, POIs, and food

Risk signal:

- Template-like phrasing repeated across plans
- Generic praise with no information value
- Placeholder text, malformed strings, or awkward sentence structure

### 5. Differentiation

Check whether the plan has a clear reason to exist as its own Explore item.

Pass signal:

- Distinct angle, mood, route focus, or audience framing
- Clear separation from other common city itineraries
- Title and summary express a recognizable concept

Risk signal:

- Feels like a default city攻略
- Lacks a memorable angle
- Only differs from other plans by superficial wording

## Reviewer Questions

Use these questions during manual review:

- Does this plan have real city character?
- Is the target traveler obvious and believable?
- Can the route actually be followed?
- Does the writing sound natural?
- Does this item feel distinct from generic攻略?

## Suggested Failure Tags

- 城市特色不足
- 太像普通攻略
- 文案模板化
- 路线不合理
- 人群匹配不足
- POI选择一般
- 美食推荐不足
- 其他

## Review Outcome Standard

A strong plan should satisfy most of the following:

- Clear audience fit
- Clear city identity
- Coherent route design
- Natural Chinese copy
- Meaningful differentiation

If two or more core dimensions are weak, the item should be treated as needing revision before being trusted as a strong Explore candidate.

## Current Schema Defects

The validation did not require schema changes, but it exposed gaps to consider after H3.5.3:

1. `city_template.schema.json` had no explicit `weather_or_seasonal_texture` object. Hangzhou benefits from rain/mist/seasonal lake cues.
2. `asset_requirements.families` could express counts, but not preferred concrete asset examples such as West Lake, People's Park, or Taikoo Li.
3. `food_backbone` existed, but Image Template v1 did not distinguish generic food mood from named dish/shop evidence. Chengdu shows this distinction matters.
4. `modern_contrast` is available as an asset family, but image slot mapping still needs human interpretation between `poi_gallery` and `day_cover`.
5. Golden review rules defined required dimensions, but not pass/fail thresholds such as minimum number of reviewed covers or maximum duplicate visual similarity.
6. Provenance requirements were field-level only; they did not express license policy or commercial-use readiness.

## H3.5.4-A Schema Refinement

H3.5.4-A adds optional fields only, so Beijing, Hangzhou, and Chengdu examples remain compatible without migration.

City Template additions:

- `city_identity.seasonal_texture`
- `asset_requirements.families[].asset_examples`
- `asset_requirements.food_evidence_level`
- `asset_requirements.golden_review_thresholds`

Image Template additions:

- `usage_profiles[].review_trigger`
- `slots[].review_trigger`
- `review_requirements.*.review_trigger`
- Structured provenance requirement objects with `required_fields`, `license_policy`, and `notes`

Resolved or reduced defects:

- Seasonal/weather texture is now representable.
- Authoring examples are now representable without becoming generated ids.
- Food evidence strictness is now representable.
- Golden review thresholds are now representable.
- Provenance policy can now express license expectations.

Still open:

- `modern_contrast` still requires human mapping between place evidence and experience cover.
- Threshold values are not enforced by a validator or pipeline.
- License acceptability is described but not automatically checked.

## Validation Result

City Template v1 and Image Template v1 are sufficiently general for a controlled next step across Beijing, Hangzhou, Chengdu, Shanghai, and Xi'an.

They are ready to enter pipeline-integration design, but not full implementation or automated 10-city generation.

Recommended boundary:

- Yes: design a template-to-content/image-manifest integration plan.
- Yes: define read-only validation tooling for examples.
- No: do not auto-generate city content yet.

- No: do not remove Golden manual review.

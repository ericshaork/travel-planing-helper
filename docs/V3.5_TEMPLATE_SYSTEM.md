# Wanderly V3.5 Template System

Status: H3.5.2 schema design only. This document does not define a generator, builder, or pipeline integration.

## Goals

The template system captures reusable product rules discovered from `trip_packages/beijing_couple_golden_v2`.

It is designed to support future city expansion such as Hangzhou, Chengdu, Shanghai, Xi'an, and other Wanderly Explore cities while keeping the current Content Pipeline, Image Pipeline, Image Decision code, manifest schema, and Trip Package Builder unchanged.

## Core Principles

- `asset_instance_id` is the stable join key between content, image requirements, image assets, and packaged files.
- City templates describe content intent and required asset coverage, not generated itinerary output.
- Image templates describe image slots, usage profiles, strategy rules, provenance requirements, and review bars.
- Final package `source` must reflect the actual origin of the image. AI-generated covers must not be marked as `real_photo`.
- Golden Sample refinement may override a planned `generation_mode` at the package artifact level, but provenance and review notes must preserve what happened.

## City Template v1

City Template v1 defines the reusable city and trip planning inputs needed before generating an ExplorePlan and Image Manifest.

Required sections:

- `template_id`: Stable id for this city/traveler template.
- `template_version`: Currently `city_template_v1`.
- `city_identity`: City code, display name, region, country, language, timezone, and visual identity.
- `traveler_profile`: Traveler type, days, season, budget, pace, motivation, theme, must-include, and avoid list.
- `content_rules`: Route quality rules, trip tone, POI logic, food logic, and hard constraints.
- `asset_requirements`: Required content asset families and minimum image coverage.
- `image_template_ref`: Reference to an Image Template v1 document.
- `golden_reference`: Optional reference to the Golden Sample package used as calibration.

Optional refinement fields added in H3.5.4-A:

- `city_identity.seasonal_texture`: seasonal or weather-linked city texture for content and image planning.
- `asset_requirements.families[].asset_examples`: authoring examples for a family. These are not generated asset ids.
- `asset_requirements.food_evidence_level`: clarifies whether food imagery is city mood, real dish evidence, or named shop/dish evidence.
- `asset_requirements.golden_review_thresholds`: minimum package-level thresholds before a city template can be treated as Golden.

### City Identity

City identity should include both operational and visual cues.

Examples:

- `code`: `beijing`, `hangzhou`, `chengdu`, `shanghai`, `xian`
- `visual_identity_keywords`: `imperial axis`, `lake garden`, `tea hills`, `street food`, `river skyline`
- `signature_moods`: `first arrival`, `slow walk`, `evening closure`, `local food memory`

`seasonal_texture` is optional but recommended when weather or season strongly shapes the Wanderly feel. Examples:

- Hangzhou: mist, spring rain, willow, lake haze, tea hills.
- Chengdu: autumn warmth, bamboo shade, teahouse steam, night-market glow.
- Shanghai: humid evening lights, plane trees, river haze.
- Xi'an: dry light, city wall dusk, loess earth tone, night-market warmth.

### Content Rules

Content rules should preserve Wanderly's product position: travel magazine plus private journal.

Default rules:

- Avoid overpacked days.
- Use route order that a first-time traveler can follow.
- Preserve traveler fit.
- Prefer concrete POIs and food experiences.
- Do not fabricate real-time prices, opening states, queues, or ticket availability.
- Keep food and place evidence grounded in real-world locations.

### Asset Requirements

City Template v1 should not hardcode Beijing asset ids. It should describe required families:

- `signature_anchor`: city-defining landmark or first-arrival place.
- `historic_texture`: neighborhood, old street, craft, local texture.
- `slow_solo` or `slow_walk`: quiet pause, reflective walk, park, riverside, or local rhythm.
- `modern_contrast`: contemporary city layer or skyline contrast.
- `evening_closure`: dusk, night walk, waterfront, light, or last-stop atmosphere.
- `food_backbone`: local food anchor when the trip profile needs it.

`asset_examples` can guide authoring with examples like `West Lake`, `People's Park teahouse`, or `Xi'an City Wall`, but examples should not be treated as generated ids.

`food_evidence_level` values:

- `none`: food imagery is not required.
- `city_mood`: generic city food mood may be acceptable, without named shop or dish claims.
- `real_dish`: real dish evidence is required.
- `named_shop_or_dish`: real photo evidence is required; AI generation is not allowed.

`golden_review_thresholds` can set minimum reviewed covers, minimum real place-gallery assets, duplicate limits, and provenance requirements.

## Image Template v1

Image Template v1 defines reusable image slots and strategy rules.

Required sections:

- `template_id`
- `template_version`
- `join_key`
- `slots`
- `usage_profiles`
- `generation_strategies`
- `provenance_requirements`
- `review_requirements`
- `golden_review_rules`

Optional refinement fields added in H3.5.4-A:

- `review_trigger`: conditions that trigger manual review, Golden refinement, or rejection.
- Structured provenance requirements with `required_fields`, `license_policy`, and notes.

### Image Slots

Recommended abstract slots:

- `explore_cover`: Explore card entrance image.
- `trip_cover`: overall route or city mood cover.
- `day_cover`: day section or experience closure cover.
- `poi_gallery`: real POI/place evidence.
- `food_gallery`: real food or restaurant evidence.
- `detail_image`: optional material, signage, street, ticket, or journal detail.

### Image Usage Profiles

- `cover`: emotional entrance, editorial quality, high review bar.
- `place`: real place evidence, reliable geography and architecture.
- `experience`: itinerary memory, mood, movement, time of day.
- `food`: real dish/shop/table evidence.

### Default Strategy Matrix

| usage_profile | default_generation_mode | source_preference | Golden refinement |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cover` | `hybrid` | `mixed` | May finalize as `ai_generated` if real photo is ordinary or off-mood |
| `place` | `real_photo` | `real_first` | Must remain real photo |
| `experience` | `hybrid` | `mixed` | May finalize as real photo or AI-generated depending on quality |
| `food` | `real_photo` | `real_first` | Must remain real photo for specific shops/dishes |

Typical `review_trigger` examples:

- Cover manual review when the real photo is a detail crop, generic stock-like image, crowded commercial scene, poor location identity, or wrong time of day.
- Place rejection when POI identity is not recognizable or provenance is missing.
- Experience review when the time-of-day mood conflicts with the itinerary.
- Food rejection when a named shop or dish is AI-generated.

## Image Decision Rules

Rules to keep:

- Food assets default to `real_photo`.
- Place/gallery assets default to `real_photo`.
- Experience assets default to `hybrid`.
- Signature POI gallery images remain `real_photo`.
- Concrete place-entry covers use `hybrid` as the planned generation mode.
- City mood covers may use `ai_generated` when no precise POI verification is required.

Rules to upgrade in future implementation:

- Add cover intent: `place_entry`, `city_mood`, `itinerary_emotion`.
- Add quality escalation for covers: if real photo is a detail crop, crowded commercial scene, poor identity match, or off-time-of-day, trigger AI cover candidate review.
- Preserve planned `generation_mode` and final `source` separately.
- Treat `image_usage_profile = place` as real-photo-only unless an explicit future exception is approved.

## Golden Review Rules

Golden review is the artifact-level quality gate before a template can guide city expansion.

Required review dimensions:

- `cover_quality`: cover images must have editorial composition, readable place identity, and Wanderly tone.
- `location_identity`: place/gallery images must show real, recognizable locations.
- `wanderly_style`: the full set should feel like travel magazine plus private journal.
- `duplicate_check`: no final asset should accidentally reuse the same image unless explicitly approved.
- `provenance_check`: AI and real-photo assets must have correct metadata.
- `remaining_notes`: explicit caveats for downstream usage.

Recommended threshold examples:

- Every `cover` asset is manually reviewed.
- A 3-day Golden package has at least 2-3 real place-gallery assets.
- Accidental duplicate final image paths are not allowed.
- Completed assets have 100% provenance coverage.
- Named food/shop images have real-photo provenance.

Recommended statuses:

- `passed`
- `passed_with_note`
- `warning`
- `failed`

## Beijing Couple Golden v2 Mapping

The first Golden Sample package demonstrates the intended split:

- Four cover/experience cover assets finalized as `ai_generated`.
- Three place gallery assets finalized as `wikimedia` real photos.
- Manifest-planned `generation_mode` remains visible, while package-level `source` records the final origin.

Key lessons:

- Cover quality is a product judgment, not only a provider-routing result.
- Real-photo gallery assets must prioritize recognizable location evidence.
- Hybrid is a strategy class, not merely a failed-provider fallback.

## Future 10-City Inputs

Every new city template should provide:

- City identity: code, name, region, country, timezone, language.
- Visual identity: landmark types, color/mood keywords, city texture, seasonal cues.
- Traveler profile: traveler type, days, budget, pace, season, motivation, theme.
- Route constraints: must include, avoid, geography grouping, day rhythm.
- Content asset candidates: family, role, district, best time, traveler fit, search keywords.
- Image requirements: slots, usage profiles, source preference, generation mode, review requirement.
- Provenance policy: acceptable real-photo sources and AI provider metadata requirements.
- Golden review policy: which assets require manual approval before expansion.

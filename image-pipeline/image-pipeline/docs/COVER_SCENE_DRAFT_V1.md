## Review Notes

Recommended grouping logic:
- Hangzhou: water-edge calm, lane texture, tea hills, rainy literary mood, bridge-mist landmark
- Chengdu: tea lifestyle, neighborhood social rhythm, gentle market life, shaded streets
- Yunnan: valley arrival, terraces, courtyard intimacy, meadow pause, craft-old-town texture

Manual review should prioritize:
- whether same-city scenes are visually distinct at thumbnail size
- whether the focal subject is clear enough for a cover card
- whether the scene feels like a travel memory instead of a tourism poster
- whether `avoid` rules are strong enough to prevent scene collapse
- whether each city has both recognizable identity and enough non-landmark variety
